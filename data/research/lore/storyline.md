# Counter-Strike Online - Zombie Saga Storyline Overview

A faithful, ordered summary of the main Counter-Strike Online (Nexon) storyline as documented on the
Counter Strike Online Wiki (cso.fandom.com, CC BY-SA 3.0). Written in our own words from the wiki's
season "Storyline" sections and organisation/character pages; every section lists its sources.
Where the wiki itself is vague or contradictory, that is noted rather than smoothed over.

> Terminology: the wiki uses **Rex Virus** (Rex Research Institute page, Dr. Rex page) and **Z-VIRUS**
> (Z-VIRUS patch page, Season 2 storyline) for the same zombie virus. "Zombie Scenario" (ZS) is the
> co-op PvE mode whose chapters carry the story; each "Season" is a set of chapters/maps.

---

## 0. Setting and the two sides

Counter-Strike Online keeps the classic CT-vs-TR framing. **Counter-Terrorists** are government-
sanctioned forces (SEAL Team 6, GSG-9, SAS, GIGN, Spetsnaz, plus CSO-added 707, SOZO, PLA, SAT, SAF).
**Terrorists** are outlaw groups (Phoenix Connexion, Elite Crew, Arctic Avengers, Guerrilla Warfare,
Midwest Militia, plus Red Beret Condottiere, Vigilante Corps, National Liberation Campaign and the
Asia Red Army). The zombie saga sits on top of this war; from the Aegis Institute onward both sides
fight the undead together.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Counter-Terrorist , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Terrorist

## 1. Origin - Dr. Rex, the Super Soldier Project and the Rex Virus

1. The **Rex Research Institute** began as doctors and scientists making life-prolonging medicine and
   drifted toward the dream of human immortality. Publicly its Rex Laboratory built US military weapons.
2. Its director, **Dr. Steve Rex** (bio- and mechanical engineer), secretly ran the **Super Soldier
   Project**: soldiers in high-tech battle gear meant to defeat the Terrorists. No human could handle
   the gear; Rex decided the human body was the bottleneck.
3. An **anonymous informer** - later revealed to be **Douglas Jacob** - sent Rex a private report about
   powerful animated corpses ("zombies") that had existed in Europe decades earlier (the corpses of
   *Heorgen*). Rex had them excavated, built a secret underground lab and studied them for months.
4. He formulated the **Rex Virus**: it painlessly amplifies the body's strongest capacities - but the
   infected become mindless zombies bent on killing every human, CT or TR alike. Since consciousness was
   required to use the battle gear, Rex declared the project a failure and sealed the lab.
5. The government stopped funding him. Douglas Jacob kept funding him in secret.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Dr._Rex , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Rex_Research_Institute ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Douglas_Jacob

## 2. Zombie Scenario: Season 1 - The Lost City outbreak (Lost City, Double Gate, Trap, Last Clue, Chaos, Dead End, Nightmare)

1. The sealed experiment breaks loose (the wiki's "Chaos" chapter is where the zombies escape the
   lab). A city - the **Lost City**, rumoured to be in Cuba - is quarantined for Rex Virus infection.
2. A research group sent into the city goes silent. A month later the government sends Special Forces
   (the *advance unit*, commander **Andrey**), who find the city crawling with zombies. Their comms are
   cut, they are attacked, and they barricade a construction site.
3. Two people in protective suits flee from them; the survivors salvage equipment and hold out. As the
   barricades fall, a voice calling himself **"Rex"** booms across the city. Andrey realises this is the
   perpetrator and leads the survivors to the rooftop the voice came from (Andrey's note).
4. Two days after the distress call a **rescue team** (signal corpsman **Felix**, Captain **Choi Ji
   Yoon**) finds Andrey and his men dead. Andrey's note includes a map pointing to a location in the city.
5. Following the **Last Clue**, the team enters the laboratory complex - far larger underground than
   above - and meets the first boss, **Prototype Phobos**. Its DNA is recovered.
6. The government orders a retreat, but Captain Choi cuts comms with HQ and asks the team to keep
   investigating the lab (Felix wonders what she is really after). In **Chaos** the **Siege Type Phobos**
   is fought, and the Siege Types are released into the world.
7. **Oberon**, which escaped the Rex basement and was captured by the government, breaks loose in
   **Dead End** (Habana Vieja / Old Havana) and is killed. **Nightmare** is an annihilation gauntlet of the
   season's bosses. Jennifer is captured in the Rex lab and kept as a living test subject.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_1 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Prototype_Phobos ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Oberon , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Rex_Research_Institute

## 3. Human Scenario / Sidewinder - the Vanguard Company appears

After the Siege Types are released in Chaos, the **Vanguard Company** - an ill-fated munitions firm
that acts against governments and sponsors terrorists - is tasked with taking the Siege Type samples to
the Vanguard Lab. Government forces under **Commander Victor** engage them, but Vanguard seizes the sample
by force and withdraws. Vanguard's Trooper/Ghost/Lancer/Titan soldiers are products of Dr. Rex's Super
Soldier project, which Vanguard supported; Dr. Rex left the company when the project completed.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_Company

## 4. Zombie Scenario: Season 2 - Hunting Dione (Toxicity, Culvert, Decoy, Angra Nest, Poisoning)

1. One year after the Lost City incident, the **Aegis Institute** (founded by the Counter-Terrorism
   Alliance to research zombies and build anti-zombie weapons) enters the story. **Senior Researcher Soy
   (Ahn Soi)** briefs the **LS team** over the Caribbean: zombie strength has waned since Lost City, but
   nobody knows whether Dr. Rex is brewing a new batch - find clues.
2. In the jungle (**Toxicity**) the team fights the reptilian **Dione**; it is wounded but escapes - the
   first boss not to die when its health is emptied. HQ orders it captured alive, or killed.
3. **Culvert**: the team corners Dione in the sewers; it hides, hordes of zombies pour in, and Dione
   evolves into **Siege Type Dione**. A lure device (which also attracts zombies) is planned - **Decoy** -
   where the team faces **Revenant**.
4. **Angra Nest**: at a ruined facility Soy determines that the Z-VIRUS-mutating Dione is in a
   defenceless chrysalis stage. The team rushes in to kill it before it emerges and instead faces its
   final winged form, **Angra**, the first flying boss.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_2 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Dione ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Angra , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Aegis_Institute

## 5. Z-VIRUS (patch) - the illegal research complex

Side-story content: **Sting Finger** (a Female Researcher - Choi Ji Yoon's sister - zombified by the
Z-VIRUS at an unidentified illegal research complex) and **Venom Guard** (a security guard from the same
complex), plus the **Forbidden** map, a disused chemical-waste site turned zombie nest. The Z-VIRUS trailer
shows Choi Ji Yoon investigating a lab and fending them off with an M32 MGL.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Z-VIRUS , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Choi_Ji_Yoon

## 6. Zombie Scenario: Season 3 - The fall of Vanguard (Contact, Behind, Threat, Panic Room; extra: Illusion)

1. Vanguard's research on the Siege Type samples goes wrong and the Vanguard Lab suffers a zombie
   outbreak; even its defence systems are infected. **Norman**, Vanguard's financier, writes an unsent
   letter reproaching **Jim** (leader of the **Red Lizard**) for choosing a side, then flees when guards
   bring urgent news.
2. **David Black** (leader of the **AFC** army; ex-SAS instructor who joined the Terrorists after the Rex
   basement) receives Norman's urgent request, loses contact, and tracks him to a deserted sandstorm town
   hiding the Vanguard Lab's secret entrance (**Contact**).
3. The AFC break into Vanguard HQ at heavy cost; **Erika** - the sole Lost City survivor David rescued and
   treats as a daughter - is wounded. Norman, watching on cameras, sends AMP Suits "in support" and David
   fortifies the munitions factory (**Behind**, **Threat**).
4. Norman's trap fails but an **AMP Suit kidnaps Erika**. David rides the lift to the **Panic Room** to
   rescue her and punish Norman; the "safest place in Vanguard" becomes the worst battlefield. Norman is
   believed to have been infected there and died. The Vanguard Lab is destroyed; David and Erika go missing.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_3 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_Company ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/David_Black , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Erika

## 7. Zombie Scenario: Season 4 - Dr. Rex revealed and destroyed (Encounter, Conspiracy, Envy Mask, Paranoia; Madness)

1. Days after Vanguard's collapse, **Gerard** (ex-SAS, now a US PMC contractor and David's former
   protege) leads a team to retrieve research samples from the ruined base and hand them to **Jim's**
   transport team in the polar regions. A soldier is infected by a sample; the aircraft crash-lands; the
   survivors escape by train and Gerard meets Jim (**Encounter**).
2. The sample is reclaimed from **Frozen Terror**; Gerard and Jim push through the polar wastes to a
   handover with a **secret client** (**Conspiracy**). The client greets them with waves of zombies.
3. The client is **Dr. Rex** (**Envy Mask**). He admits the Super Soldier Program produced zombies and a
   viral plague instead of super soldiers - and hints that someone else is continuing his research.
4. Infected, Rex mutates into a gigantic zombie combining earlier bosses' traits and attacks
   (**Paranoia**) and is fought again as the final boss of **Madness**. He is the first mutant to keep his
   consciousness - a trait all later bosses share.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_4 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Dr._Rex ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Gerard

## 8. Zombie Scenario: Season 5 - Jennifer, Jack and the first name-drop of Kronos (Omen, Memories, Another Truth, Last Ride; Third Report)

1. **Jennifer** learns the Super Soldier Project continues even after Rex's lab was destroyed. Haunted by
   nightmares of the Lost City, she leads her special-forces team back there and finds only the walking
   dead (**Omen**).
2. Shocked by the **Laser Wing** incident, she learns other forces are pursuing the project without
   understanding the risk. In the lab facilities she meets a huge, strangely familiar zombie - **Jack**, the
   man she left behind in the Rex accident (**Memories**).
3. She overhears someone contacting Jack by radio and seeks them out; a stranger offers "another truth"
   while she is torn between truth and revenge for Jack (**Another Truth**).
4. About to learn the truth from **Soi**, she is attacked by a new "cronobotic" enemy (**Crono Wing**) and
   risks her life to protect her team (**Last Ride**). Crono Wing and Soi reveal that **Douglas Jacob**,
   chairman of **Kronos**, funded Dr. Rex's Super Soldier Project. (Douglas Jacob page adds: it was
   Douglas who "rescued" Jack after **Yuri** bombed the Rex facility, made him Laser Wing - the first
   conscious zombie - and turned him against Jennifer by claiming she abandoned him.)
5. **Third Report** (extra): Soy is escorted by a hidden NPC who turns out to be David Black.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_5 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Douglas_Jacob ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/David_Black

## 9. Zombie Scenario: Season 6 - Four episodes (Episode Choi, Episode Victor, Episode Lucia, Episode Carlito)

- **Episode Choi**: the CIA sends **Choi Ji Yoon** to infiltrate the **Asia Red Army**; **Ritsuka** exposes
  her and holds her hostage; agent **May** is sent to rescue her. The ARA, led by **Mr. X**, is revealed to
  be funded by Douglas Jacob to deploy a biological weapon that creates monsters and zombies.
- **Episode Victor**: the disappearance of **Victor's** squad while transporting Phobos' DNA through the
  Desert Storm operation area; Vanguard troops appear (unfought) at the start.
- **Episode Lucia**: **Lucia's** fleet, grounded by bad weather, receives a distress signal from an
  approaching supply ship; bringing it in for inspection begins a nightmare. Kronos containers holding
  **Deimos** and **Ganymede** - meant to spread the virus - are discovered.
- **Episode Carlito**: veteran Vanguard mercenary **Carlito** takes a mission directly from Douglas to
  steal data from a lab that has become a zombie lair. It is revealed Vanguard was a **child company of
  Kronos**, that Douglas orchestrated Vanguard's destruction, and that he now sabotages Carlito's unit -
  the last Vanguards - with arranged zombie attacks. Carlito survives and joins the allied forces.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_6 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Douglas_Jacob ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Kronos , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Asia_Red_Army

## 10. Zombie Scenario: Season 7 - Striking back at Kronos (All-Out, Rendezvous, Rex Labs, Lowdown)

1. **All-Out**: Carlito breaks Vanguard's bond with Kronos and, learning Kronos is raiding a secret
   research complex, leads his squad in a vengeful counter-assault. Riding the **Blaze** across the desert
   toward Kronos, the convoy is bombed by the **XT-300 Goliath** and knocked off course; government forces
   and Aegis researchers face a huge unidentified Kronos army. (Choi Ji Yoon, May, Ritsuka, Victor, Soy,
   Carlito, Jennifer, Gerard and Jim all appear.)
2. **Rendezvous**: Lucia's fleet traces repeated signals to an unidentified facility linked to her senior
   **Michaela**; yesterday's enemy becomes today's ally against a common foe. (More Deimos/Ganymede
   containers.)
3. **Rex Labs**: Erika, free of the Panic Room, suffers periodic unexplained pain. **Soi** tells David to
   bring her to the Rex Labs for a cure; they find the lab already attacked and search it for clues.
4. **Lowdown**: following coordinates from the lab, David and Erika meet **Gerard**, who reveals Douglas
   Jacob as the hidden hand behind Rex and behind Erika's kidnapping (she was a potential antibody test
   subject; infected during the abduction, her genes resist mutation at the cost of chronic pain that
   Douglas's data can cure). The three decide their next move - as Douglas reaches for them.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_7 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Douglas_Jacob

## 11. Zombie Scenario: Season 8 - Hunters and rifts (Upcoming Threat, Neon City, Abandoned Hound, Overflow)

1. **Upcoming Threat**: David, Erika and Gerard form a new team and go looking for the person who blew
   up the Rex Labs in the past (the Season 8 page says "the man"; the Yuri and Douglas Jacob pages name
   **Yuri** as the Rex bomber). At an abandoned munitions factory Erika's pain recurs; Douglas sends a hunter
   to wipe them out.
2. **Neon City**: **Queen** and her party escape a laboratory by helicopter, are shot down onto a rooftop in
   Kronos's planned experimental city - dazzling lights, no people - and walk into a trap with a mysterious
   monster.
3. **Abandoned Hound**: Ritsuka, sent by Douglas with super-soldier support to stop David's party, fails,
   loses contact with Douglas and retreats to the Asia Red Army hideout - now in ruins.
4. **Overflow**: **Dominique** and **Alin** travel to a rendezvous point to help Yuri and find a village
   riddled with strange rifts. Ritsuka is captured by Alin and Dominique, cuts ties with Kronos and decides
   to help Carlito.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_8 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Douglas_Jacob ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Yuri

## 12. Zombie Scenario: Season 9 - The fall of Douglas Jacob (Obstacle, Secret Plan, Convoy, Chaser)

1. Alin and Dominique destroy Kronos's secret lab; the shockwave from its core exposes the locations of
   Kronos's hidden facilities, world governments investigate, and Douglas's former allies turn on him.
   He lashes out at his most important enemies at once and captures the **Aegis Institute's
   laboratories**; **Jennifer** and **Dr. Soi** arm themselves to retake them (**Obstacle**).
2. Intelligence recovered after recapturing the research centre reveals Douglas's very dangerous
   **Secret Plan**, powered by the weapons of his military factories worldwide; Soi asks outside teams to
   destroy the factory and find the hidden research facility (**Secret Plan**).
3. While Soi, Jennifer, David Black and other heroes fight Douglas, the zombies he has released overrun
   cities worldwide. The government forms special relief forces; one answers a distress call from
   survivors holding out in a temporary shelter (**Convoy**).
4. After foiling the attempt at the secret facility, the party learns **Mammon** escaped and tracks it
   (**Chaser**). (Douglas Jacob page: he had sent Mammon to destroy the disguised research facility that held
   the cure data for Erika.)
5. **Finale** (Douglas Jacob page): Douglas throws all his agents at the **Aegis Allied Force**, then
   pilots the **Adamant** battlegear himself (built from leaked Aegis combat data; a virus-infused
   **Adamant-Z** form). Defeated, he retreats to launch an ICBM; Soi calls in air support and his control
   room is bombed just in time. Douglas Jacob dies. Erika is placed under treatment.

Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Zombie_Scenario:_Season_9 , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Douglas_Jacob ,
https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Aegis_Institute , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Kronos

## 13. Side threads that touch the main plot

- **Anemone and Mirage / Krono World (2021).** Twin sisters on the VR platform *Krono World*: Mirage, a
  star streamer and secret hacker, finds a suspicious log proving Krono World's creators are running a
  scheme and tries to reach the **Aegis Lab** while enemies close in; her twin **Anemone**, a pro gamer,
  guards her. The wiki has no dedicated *Krono World* page, and the name's similarity to *Kronos* is not
  commented on by the wiki. Sources: https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Anemone ,
  https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Mirage_(Character) , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Alpaca_Anemone
- **Karin and Milia (2024).** Fairies from another world who followed **Skadi** and **Nyx** (2022
  transcendent classes) through a strange light into the CSO world. Sources:
  https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Karin , https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Milia
- **Kronos operatives.** Alice (Kronos defence system), Cyborg (living weapon), Angelina (super soldier
  via a Kronos serum), Amy (unstable android), Agent Ritsuka, Pyromaniac Yuri. Source:
  https://cso.fandom.com/wiki/Kronos
- **Zombie Shelter / Zombie Giant.** Several bosses (Phobos, Oberon, Angra, Dione) reappear as message
  portraits in Zombie Shelter: Coop and as playable giants in Zombie Giant mode - gameplay reuse rather
  than new story. Sources: boss pages above.
